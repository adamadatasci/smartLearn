Earth at the Center

Assume Earth is the center of inertia for this lesson. Watch the Sun and Moon move around Earth, while the Sun makes one apparent turn every 24 hours. The small screens show day/night, the Moon in the local sky, moon phases, and seasons with snow, flowers, sun, and leaves.
